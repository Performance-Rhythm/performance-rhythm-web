## Priority 0 — Fix Before Calling the Public Site Finished

### 1. Replace the eight Method-page image placeholders

Owner decision needed: approve a visual direction, then create or select two visuals for each Method step: Notice, Reset, Strengthen, and Repeat.

Acceptance criteria:

- No public panel says `[Placeholder Image]`.
- Images or diagrams clarify the method instead of functioning as decoration.
- Visual style matches the premium, human, scientific brand; avoid generic meditation, medical ECG, and stock-office clichés.
- Each image has useful alternative text and is compressed for the web.

### 2. Commission a qualified legal review

The Privacy Policy and Terms contain legal and operational representations that should not be approved solely as website copy. Counsel should review at minimum:

- CCPA and GDPR applicability and rights language.
- Analytics, cookies, IP-address collection, and Do Not Track statements against the actual deployed setup.
- Workshop survey data, optional participant email collection, retention, deletion, access, and de-identification practices.
- Health and safety disclaimers for breathing, meditation, and movement practices.
- Arbitration, class-action waiver, limitation of liability, cancellation, refund, and payment terms.
- The promise to respond to privacy and terms inquiries within 30 days.
- Whether `contact@performancerhythm.com` should be the designated legal/privacy contact.

### 3. Approve public pricing and packaging

The corrected site now aligns the Leadership Program range with the internal pricing strategy, but the entire public pricing presentation needs an owner decision:

- Confirm whether Signature Workshop pricing should remain public at $750–$2,000.
- Confirm Leadership Programs at $5,000–$20,000 per program.
- Confirm Corporate Programs at $20,000–$100,000+ or replace ranges with customized-proposal language.
- Define what is included, what affects scope, travel policy, and whether Founding Partner pricing is still available.

### 4. Verify the platform promises against the actual roadmap

The Programs page promises a web/mobile subscription platform, company-specific content, progress tracking, and engagement metrics. Confirm which features are committed, which are exploratory, and whether "Beta access available to Founding Partner clients" is currently true.

## Priority 1 — Credibility and Conversion

Select one and add it beside the founder section, then confirm whether Jordan should appear anywhere on the public site; Programs currently mentions direct access to both Shane and Jordan.

### 6. Decide whether to publish Login and Dashboard links

The footer currently links to public "Coming Soon" Login and Dashboard pages. They are excluded from search indexing, but they can make the product look unfinished. Hide them until the member platform is usable, or replace the Platform footer column with useful live links.

### 7. Verify every public contact path

- Confirm `shane@performancerhythm.com` and `contact@performancerhythm.com` are both monitored.
- Confirm the public phone number should remain visible.
- Confirm the 30-minute Cal.com event and Founding Partner event are active and have correct availability, intake questions, confirmation copy, and branding.
- Decide whether the site needs a real contact form. If added, implement spam protection, delivery monitoring, consent language, and Privacy Policy updates together.

### 8. Add proof without inflating claims

The site explains the method well but has little external proof. Add, when available:

- Named client testimonials with written permission.
- Short case studies with context, participant count, delivery format, measures, results, and limitations.
- Aggregate workshop survey findings only after defining minimum sample size and privacy rules.
- Facilitator credentials with verifiable issuing organization and current status.

### 9. Establish a research-maintenance cadence

- Review institutional surveys annually and peer-reviewed sections twice per year.
- Record the last review date on the Research page.
- Check for retractions, corrected articles, moved URLs, and stronger systematic reviews.
- Keep descriptions conservative; distinguish association, short-term state change, and causal evidence.
- Do not describe a PubMed abstract page as open-access full text.

### 10. Improve long-page navigation

Research, Programs, Method, Privacy, and Terms are long pages. Add a compact table of contents or section-jump navigation, beginning with Research and Method.

### 11. Complete accessibility QA

- Test keyboard navigation, focus visibility, mobile-menu behavior, heading order, link purpose, contrast, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Validate the eight future Method images with useful alt text.
- Run automated accessibility checks, then perform a manual keyboard and screen-reader pass.

### 12. Confirm measurement language and operating practice

The site now uses more responsible outcome language. Document the actual measurement protocol behind claims: measures used, timing, consent, comparison method, minimum sample size, and how results are reported to organizations.

- Create per-page Open Graph images and confirm LinkedIn previews.
- Review page titles and descriptions after the positioning edits.

### 16. Review the favicon/app-icon system

Inspect the current SVG icon at small sizes and confirm the mark is not clipped. Produce the complete approved favicon and touch-icon set if any size fails.

### 17. Add operational monitoring

- Schedule an automated internal-link and external-link check.
- Add uptime monitoring for the public site and staff workshop manager.
- Monitor failed bookings, email delivery, and workshop API errors.
- Define who receives alerts and what response time is expected.

### 18. Maintain a content-release checklist

Before publishing new pages or claims, check spelling, mobile layout, link destinations, evidence quality, image rights, alt text, metadata, analytics, legal implications, and owner approval for pricing or product commitments.
