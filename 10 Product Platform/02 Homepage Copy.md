# SECTION 9

## Meet The Founder

Shane Curtis spent more than 17 years leading teams in healthcare technology across sales, marketing, customer success, customer service, and implementation.

Throughout his career, he observed a recurring challenge.

Many talented employees and leaders were not struggling because they lacked skill, intelligence, or motivation.

They were struggling because chronic stress was quietly undermining their capacity to perform, communicate, lead, and thrive.

After years of studying human performance, breathwork, nervous system regulation, leadership development, and resilience training, Shane recognized one of the greatest opportunities in organizational development:

Helping organizations strengthen the people responsible for creating their success.

Today, he combines executive leadership experience with practical human capacity development to help organizations build stronger leaders, healthier cultures, sustainable performance, and meaningful growth.
