# Website Evidence Library

Last reviewed: August 20, 2026

## Purpose

This document records the evidence standard and topic structure used by the public Research page. The website source remains the exact source of truth for titles, descriptions, and links.

## Evidence Standard

- Prefer peer-reviewed meta-analyses, systematic reviews, and well-designed primary studies.
- Use current workforce surveys from recognized organizations when population-level context is useful.
- Use government and intergovernmental frameworks for occupational-health definitions and action guidance.
- Describe what the evidence supports, including important limitations.
- Do not convert association into causation or individual outcomes into guaranteed organizational results.
- Do not imply that individual regulation practices replace healthy work design, adequate resources, recovery opportunities, or clinical care.
- Review institutional surveys annually and all other sections at least twice per year.

## Maintenance Checklist

1. Verify each link resolves to the intended title and publisher.
2. Check for retractions, expressions of concern, corrections, or stronger newer reviews.
3. Confirm the public description matches the source's actual design and findings.
4. Preserve limitations where risk of bias, heterogeneity, or low certainty is material.
5. Update the visible review date when a meaningful review is completed.
6. Record any source changes in the website audit and session memory.
